About Gas Busters

Founded in 1998, Gas Busters has been at the forefront of offshore petrochemical detection technology. Our team of engineers, data scientists, and safety experts bring unparalleled expertise to every project.

We combine cutting-edge technology with decades of field experience to identify and mitigate risks before they impact operations, personnel, or the environment.
